# Samples

The v4 score is played on real orchestral samples, all released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)** by Versilian Studios.
No attribution is required; credit is given anyway. Download them with `python tools/fetch_orchestra.py`
(the exact file list is `tools/orchestra_files.txt`); they are not committed.

- **VSCO 2 Community Edition** (https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE): violin, viola, cello and contrabass sections
  (sustain, spiccato, tremolo, pizzicato), French horn, tenor trombone, tuba.
- **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** (https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL): Steinway B grand piano,
  timpani, concert bass drums, toms, modern snare, clash and suspended cymbals, gong.

Everything else (sine kick, sub booms, whooshes, risers, the hall reverb) is synthesised in `music.py`.
