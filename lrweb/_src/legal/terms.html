5. What a care plan includes

Every plan includes managed hosting, an SSL certificate, WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, daily off-site backups, security and uptime monitoring, and email support. Higher plans add included edit time, performance work, priority response and reporting, as set out on the pricing page and confirmed in writing when you join.

What counts as a small edit

Small edits are routine content changes to an existing page: updating text, prices or opening hours, swapping a photo, adding a news post, fixing a typo. Each is the sort of thing that takes minutes rather than hours.

The amount of edit time included is set by your plan and confirmed in writing when you join. Anything beyond it — new pages, redesigned sections, new features or functionality, content migration, or work on a site we don't host — is quoted at £60 per hour, with no VAT to add while we are not VAT registered, and agreed with you before we start. We'd rather tell you a job is chargeable than quietly absorb it and resent it.

Response targets

Response targets stated on your plan are the times we aim to reply within on working days, not guaranteed resolution times. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding English public holidays. Genuine emergencies — a site down or compromised — are dealt with as emergencies, whatever day it is.

6. Your right to cancel if you're a consumer

This section applies only if you're buying as a consumer, meaning wholly or mainly outside your trade, business, craft or profession. Most of our clients are businesses and organisations, in which case section 7 applies instead and this section doesn't.

Because we agree our contracts by email, phone or in person away from business premises, the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 give you a right to cancel.

  • You have 14 days from the day after the contract is made to cancel, for any reason, without giving one.
  • To cancel, just tell us clearly in writing — an email to support@lrweb.uk saying you want to cancel is enough. You don't need a form, and we'll acknowledge it the same working day where we can.
  • If you cancel and we haven't started, you get a full refund within 14 days, by the same method you paid.
  • If you ask us to start within the 14 days, we will — but you'll be liable for a proportionate amount for the work done up to the moment you cancel. We'll set that out clearly and refund the rest of your deposit within 14 days.
  • Once a service has been fully performed within the 14 days at your express request, and you've acknowledged that you lose your cancellation right on completion, the right to cancel ends.

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights, including your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 to have services carried out with reasonable care and skill, within a reasonable time, and for a reasonable price where no price was agreed.
