Two jobs: build it well, then look after it.

Everything we offer fits into one of those two. No bolt-on marketing packages, no mystery line items.

The ongoing bit

Managed hosting & care

This is the heart of LRWeb. Your website becomes our responsibility, permanently, for a flat monthly fee. Most of this happens at unsociable hours so it never gets in your way. You’ll mostly notice nothing. That’s the point.

Managed hosting

Fast, secure servers we run ourselves, powered by 100% renewable energy.

Software updates

Daily backups

Stored off-site, separate from the server, restorable on request.

Security monitoring

Malware scanning, firewall rules, login protection.

Uptime monitoring

We usually know your site is down before you do.

SSL certificates

Renewed automatically, padlock always on.

Performance care

Caching, image optimisation and speed tuning.

Small content edits

Prices, hours, photos, wording. Just email us.

Real support

Questions answered by the people who built your site.

Moving an existing site to us

Switching hosts, minus the headache.

New websites

How a build runs

From first email to launch.

Five steps, a fixed price, and you always know what happens next.

Start a conversation

  1. 01

    The conversation

    You tell us about your business, what you like, what you hate, and what the website needs to do. We listen more than we talk.

  2. 02

    A clear quote

    Fixed price, written down, with exactly what’s included. A 50% deposit secures your slot and work begins.

  3. 03

    Design options

    You see two or three real design directions for your homepage: not mood boards, actual designs. You pick one and tell us what to change.

  4. 04

    The build

    We build the full site, write or polish the copy, sort the photos, and handle all the technical setup. Two rounds of revisions are included.

  5. 05

    Launch & handover

    Final balance settled, site goes live, then it moves onto a care plan as stated in your quote, after any included hosting period ends. You get a plain-English guide to what you own and how it works.
