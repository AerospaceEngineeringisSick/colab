    Step one · Day 1, 9am

    Mix slowly.

    Flour from a Cotswolds mill, Bristol tap water, salt and Doris — our 7-year-old starter. That’s the whole list. No yeast, no improvers, no shortcuts.

    Step two · Every 30 minutes

    Fold gently.

    Instead of kneading, we stretch and fold the dough by hand through the afternoon. It builds strength without knocking out the air.

    Step three · Overnight

    Prove patiently.

    Shaped loaves rest in the cold for 16 hours. The wild yeasts get to work, developing that deep, tangy flavour you can’t fake.

    Step four · Day 3, 5am

    Bake boldly.

    Into a 250°C deck oven with a blast of steam. We push the bake darker than most — that’s where the flavour lives.

    Our story

    It started with a jar of starter, a borrowed oven and far too many failed loaves.

    Crumb & Kiln began in 2019 as a Saturday-only hatch on Mill Row. We sold out of forty loaves in an hour, panicked, and baked eighty the next week.

    Today we’re a small team of six bakers who still do everything the slow way — long ferments, hand shaping and a stubborn refusal to compromise on flour. Our leftover bread goes to Bristol food banks every evening, so nothing goes to waste.

    Come for a loaf. Stay for a flat white and a cardamom knot. Everyone does.

    — Maya & Tom, founders
