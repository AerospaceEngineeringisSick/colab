(03) How a building comes together

Designed from the ground up.

  1. 01
    Site

    We spend time on site before we draw a line: the ground, the trees, the neighbours, the wind.

  2. 02
    Structure

    Honest structure, usually timber, sized to use as little carbon as possible.

  3. 03
    Envelope

    Airtight, well insulated and wrapped in materials that age gracefully.

  4. 04
    Light

    Windows placed for views and daylight first, heat loss second, then balanced.

(04) Principles

Good architecture is patient. It uses less, lasts longer and gets better with age.

Reuse first

The greenest building is often the one already standing. We always ask whether we can adapt before we demolish.

Build light

Timber frames, lime renders and low-carbon concrete. We measure embodied carbon on every project, from day one.

Design with people

Clients, neighbours and the people who’ll use the building are part of the design team, not an afterthought.
