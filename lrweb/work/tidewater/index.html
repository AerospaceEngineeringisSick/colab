Since 2011

We cook whatever the St Ives boats bring in: simply, generously and never more than a few hours after it’s landed.

No freezers full of prawns, no fish flown in from halfway round the world. If the weather keeps the boats in, the menu gets smaller. We think that’s how a seafood restaurant should work.
